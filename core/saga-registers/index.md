## Summary

The SAGA-specific chipset registers of the Apollo V4, converted from the official SAGA register documentation: video (chunky plane, colour ports, PIP, pointer), Arne audio, Maggie 3D, input and system registers. Classic OCS/ECS/AGA registers are not repeated here.

## Details

### About the original

- Original: [`sources/official/saga-registers/`](../../sources/official/saga-registers/), an HTML register table (`index.htm`) with one page per register. It is leading; these files are generated from it by `tools/intake/sagaregs.py`.
- Valid for Core 12000+ (WDrijver, 2026-11-04).
- `index.htm` is the current table. `saga.htm` is an older table with another audio and sprite layout; it is not used.
- The Atari section of `index.htm` is skipped.

### Scope: SAGA-specific registers only

Every one of the 419 rows of the original is marked SAGA, because SAGA contains the classic chipset. These files cover the 188 SAGA-specific rows: the rows where OCS, ECS and AGA are empty, plus the SAGA addresses that the original also marks as classic.

For every other register, the Amiga Hardware Reference Manual applies, with one difference: **on SAGA, all classic pointer registers (BPLxPT, SPRxPT, COPxLC, BLTxPT, AUDxLC, DSKPT) are 32 bits wide and reach all RAM** (WDrijver, 2026-11-04). Their pages in the original still say 18 bits. FMODE (`$DFF1FC`) bit 4 "SAGA" enables the SAGA features (original).

Wrong or missing page links in the original: the 32-bit Arne rows link to the classic Paula pages (`audxlch.htm`, `audxvol.htm`, `audxdat.htm`), the PIP rows to a page that doesn't exist. The converter uses `audxl.htm`, `audxlen.htm`, `audnvol.htm`, `audxctrl.htm` and `audxper.htm` for Arne.

### Open questions

1. The Copper form `dc.w $8380,$00FF,$0000` in the colour port pages: how the SAGA Copper encodes a 32-bit MOVE and which SAGA registers it reaches.
2. "IRQ $48" (Ethernet) and "IRQ $50" (Arne channels 4–15): vector offsets or vector numbers, and at which interrupt level.
3. Read-back at `$DFExxx`: known for `$DFE1D0`/`$DFE1D2` (pointer) and `$DFE1F4`/`$DFE1EC` (mode, frame pointer). Does every write register read back there?
4. PIP format: the original lists YUV, ApolloLib supports only `$01`–`$03`.
5. Sprites 8–15: the collision bits name them; the original documents 8 sprites.

## Differences from AmigaOS / AROS / 68060

- SAGA contains the classic chipset at its classic addresses. The registers here are added on top of it, mostly at `$DFF1E6`–`$DFF1F4`, `$DFF200`–`$DFF29F` and `$DFF380`–`$DFF4FF`.
- Classic pointers are 18 (OCS) or 21 (ECS/AGA) bits; on SAGA they are 32 bits.

## Do / Don't

- DO look up a classic register in the HRM, and its SAGA-specific registers here.
- DON'T use a register listed under the open questions without checking it on real hardware.
